# Cyberpunk Ninja

## Instructions
Click the green flag, press any key at the title screen, and choose "New Game" (or pick a chapter from "Chapters"). Kanshi Dynamics owns the Kanshi Spire and everything below it, and its CEO, the Daimyo, is building an army by tearing the souls out of the city's people. Kuro, his bodyguard, turned on him and was split in two: his body became Mukuro, captain of the hollowed guard, and his soul was thrown down the Spire. It should have faded. Instead it fell into the Sump Scrapyard and woke a scrapped Kanshi robot: Hotaru. She believes she's a person who lost her memories. He only knows someone at the top of the Spire killed him. They climb together through the Prologue, Chapter 1: Neon Alley (boss: Big Oni of the Hannya Boys), Chapter 2: The Sewers (boss: Dobunushi), Chapter 3: The Pachinko Vaults (boss: Lady Luck), Chapter 4: The Physic Gardens (boss: the Harmacist), Chapter 5: Monorail Ascent (boss: Raijin, an armored locomotive), Chapter 6: Hollow Floor (boss: Kuro himself, inside the space they share), Chapter 7: The Academy (boss: Mukuro) and Chapter 8: The Tenshu (final boss: the Daimyo), followed by the epilogue.

At the end of the Kuro fight you make the choice that decides the rest of the game: press E to reach out and compromise with him, or attack to destroy his mind and climb alone. Chapters 7 and 8, the ending and the epilogue play out differently on each path.

CONTROLS:
- Arrow keys or A / D to run. Up or W to jump (hold for a higher jump). Jump into a wall to cling to it, then jump again to wall jump. Thin platforms can be jumped up through. To drop down through one, double-tap Down or Phase Step downward (Down + Space).
- Left click (or X / J) to attack. Keep pressing to chain a 4-hit combo. Up + attack is a rising Launcher.
- Space (or Z / K) to Phase Step: an invincible dash that passes through lasers and attacks. Hold the arrow keys to aim it, including diagonals and downward. You have 2 charges, shown by the color of Hotaru's scarf and the pips under her health bar; they refill when you land or perfectly deflect.
- Attack during or right after a Phase Step for a Heavy Strike, which breaks guards and builds a lot of enemy Stance.
- Down (or S / L) to deflect right before a hit lands. Hold it to block, but blocking builds Hotaru's own Stance (the gold bar at the bottom). If her Stance fills, it breaks: she overheats and is defenseless for a moment. Press Up to jump out of a block, or Space to dash out of it.
- R to drink from the Nano-Gourd and heal. E to talk and to rest at Shrine Terminals. E, Enter or Space advances dialogue. P to pause.
- Scratch can't detect the Shift or Esc keys, so Phase Step is on Space and Pause is on P.

TIPS:
- Enemies flash white just before an attack lands: that's the moment to deflect.
- Every fighter has a Stance bar, Hotaru included. It starts empty and fills gold under pressure (deflects, Heavy Strikes, returned projectiles), then slowly settles. When an enemy's Stance fills and breaks, a red reticle appears: attack near it to perform an Execution. Regular enemies die instantly, and bosses lose a whole health segment. Every Execution also refills one gourd charge and clears Hotaru's own Stance.
- A red diamond over an enemy means a Breach attack, which can't be blocked or deflected. Phase Step through it (or jump over shockwaves).
- Shrine Terminals (the hacked vending machines) heal you, refill your gourd, and become your respawn point.
- In the Sewers, glowing runoff burns: jump it or Phase Step across. Each room starts at low tide. When the pumps gurgle and the TIDE RISING warning flashes at the bottom of the screen, the sludge is about to rise; get up high and wait it out. Drainlings leap at you from mid range and spit sludge from afar (deflect the spit back).
- Dobunushi's arms rear up and slam down: deflect the slam to pin the arm, then cut it until its Stance breaks. With both arms cut, its core opens until they regrow. The tide rises in waves through the fight, and in phase 2 it spews a Breach wave along the floor.
- In The Vaults, laser grids pulse on and off and flicker just before they switch on. Wait for a gap or Phase Step through them.
- Lady Luck's slot machine decides her next attack. When the reels stop, the machine names the move ("CARD FAN!", "SHOCKWAVE!", "LASERS!"...).
- In the Physic Gardens, step into the greenhouse heat vents to ride them upward, steering left and right as you rise. Some vents pulse, so ride them while the heat is strong.
- Garden Wardens guard your normal attacks and counter if you keep swinging into their guard. Deflect their strikes (or Heavy Strike them) to build their Stance, then attack while they recover.
- The Harmacist keeps her distance and lobs vials: deflect them back at her, and stay out of the poison clouds where they break (they sting but don't stagger you). She fends off blows outside her openings (after a throw, after a deflected slash, or while she heals). When she pulls out a stim to heal, interrupt her with a Heavy Strike. In phase 2 she's faster, throws three vials at a time, and bursts a poison cloud around herself as a Breach.
- On the monorail roofs a headwind pushes you back, harder in the air. Gaps between cars are bottomless, but the couplers below can catch you. Watch the right edge of the screen for a warning sign: a Kanshi patrol crow is about to skim the roof, so jump it, Phase Step through it, or stand on a raised deck.
- Raijin's reactor core is shuttered except while it vents steam. Deflect its cannon shells to send them straight back into the core, deflect (or jump) its pantograph arm, step off roof sections that spark before they go live, and when you hear the horn in phase 2, jump the plow it drags across the roof.
- In Hollow Floor, glitch blocks phase in and out. Absent blocks show as a faint dotted outline; the incoming set lights up and turns solid just before the other set vanishes, so wait for the outline to fill, then jump. When the screen edges turn purple, Kuro has your controls and left and right are swapped (deflect and Phase Step still work).
- Kuro fights with your own moves: your 4-hit string, blink strikes from behind, and a Breach Phase Cut. When he Phase Cuts at you, Phase Step straight through him at the same moment to Clash, which breaks his Stance and leaves him open.
- The Academy's hollowed trainees dash in and throw kunai (deflect them back). Its training holograms phase like the glitch blocks.
- Mukuro duels like Kuro: he guards everything except right after his combos. Deflect each strike to build his Stance, punish the openings with two or three hits (greed gets countered), and watch for held strikes. In phase 2 he adds a kunai fan, a diving slam that sends shockwaves along the floor (jump them), and a shadow that strikes from behind you.
- The Daimyo fights in three forms. His samurai exoskeleton swings a nodachi: deflect the iaido draw and the 3-slash strings, jump the stomp's shockwaves, and Phase Step through the thruster charge. Then the damaged armor plugs into the Tenshu: deflect the wall turrets' rounds and they fly back into it, and strike when it drops to the floor to recharge. Finally he steps out of the armor and duels you with Tamakiri: deflect his cuts (one of them is a feint), keep deflecting through his long combo strings (he flashes white before every cut, and in one string he blinks behind you between cuts; deflect the heavy last cut to stagger him), and Phase Step through the soul grab, which fills your Stance if it lands. If you spared Kuro, he fights beside you in his old body for the first two forms: he flanks the armor, deflects, Phase Steps and cuts into its openings, and each Memory Shard you found lets him get back up once (you'll see the shard burn above him).
- Nine hidden Memory Shards unlock fragments of Kuro's past. One is up through the ceiling of the Gauntlet, one is behind a breakable ceiling in the Ambush room, one is in the Overflow Cistern above the Pump Station in the Sewers, one is in a back room above the High Rollers floor in The Vaults, one is at the top of the pulsing heat vent in the Drying Lofts, one is in the Maintenance Bay behind the door in the Spiral Junction's tower wall, one is in the Locked Memory above the Possession room, one is in the Instructor's Loft above the Sparring Ring, and one is in the Daimyo's Study above the Defense Grid.
- Before each boss, the hint at the top of the screen is labeled with the boss waiting in the next room.
- Options include Assist Mode toggles (wider deflect window, slower game speed, slower Stance buildup, infinite gourd, invincibility), music and sound volume, and screen shake. Starting Chapter 7 or 8 from the chapter select uses the branch you chose last (Kuro spared, if you haven't chosen yet).

## Notes and Credits
The complete "Cyberpunk Ninja": the Prologue, all eight chapters and the epilogue, with two branches after the Kuro fight. The characters, tilesets, backdrops, UI and the Shinobi Mono font come from the Cyberpunk Ninja asset pack. The art the pack doesn't cover (the Sewers, Vaults, monorail, Hollow Floor and Academy environments, Dobunushi, the Harmacist, Lady Luck's slot machine, Raijin, the Daimyo in and out of his armor, the chapter cards and other props) was drawn or re-colored for this project in the pack's 34-color palette. The music (synthwave with shamisen and taiko) and all sound effects were synthesized from scratch for this project.

The project is generated by the scripts in `build/`: game logic is written in a Python-syntax mini-language and compiled to Scratch 3 blocks, rooms are rendered from the pack's tilesets, and the audio is synthesized with numpy. All dialogue portraits come from `art/portraits.png`, a sheet of 40x40 cells (the cell order and which speakers use each portrait are listed in `art/portraits.json`; `art/portraits_preview.png` is an enlarged, labeled copy); edit the sheet and rebuild to change them. `build/export_portraits.py` refreshes the preview from the sheet; `--hotaru art/src/hotaru.png` installs Hotaru's portraits from an 80x40 neutral/visor-up pair (her angry face is derived from the neutral one), `--kuro art/src/kuro_neutral.png art/src/kuro_smirk.png` installs Kuro's (his figure is reduced to a fixed six-color palette and set on a dark green gradient), and `--reset` rebuilds the sheet from the original pack art. All of the dialogue comes from `story.json`, which also documents the plot, cast, branches, bosses and mechanics; edit it and rebuild to change what the characters say. To rebuild, run `CN_PACK=/path/to/CyberpunkNinja_AssetPack python3 build/build.py` (needs Pillow, numpy, scipy and ffmpeg).
