# Ender's Game: Battle School

## Instructions
Click the green flag to start, then choose either "Adventure" mode, which features 6 pre-designed missions, or "Freeplay," endless randomly generated scenarios that increase in difficulty as you go. See how many you can win!

CONTROLS:
- Click and drag a box around your ships to select them. Click one of your ships to select just that ship.
- With ships selected, click open space to move there, or click a Formic ship to attack it. Ships fire on their own at any enemy in range. Asteroids block movement and weapons fire, so you can use them as cover.
- Press A to select all your ships, or 1, 2 or 3 to select all fighters, cruisers or dreadnoughts, respectively. Press S to stop the selected ships while they're moving.
- Press P to pause (and Q while paused to quit to the menu). Press M to turn the music on or off.
- Click or press space to advance dialogue.

SHIP TYPES:
- Fighter: Fast and fragile, with short-range rapid lasers.
- Cruiser: Slower and tougher, with longer-range guns.
- Dreadnought: Very slow and heavily armored, with long-range shells.
The Formics have fast drones, heavier warships, and hive ships which keep breeding drones until they're destroyed.
