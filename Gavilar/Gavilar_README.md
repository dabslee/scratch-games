# Tetris

## Instructions
Click the green flag to start, then read the "How to Play" card for instructions. Once you click the "Play" button, fight through the palace to the king before the 10-minute timer runs out.

## Notes and Credits
"Szeth-son-son-Vallano wore white on the day he was to kill a king."

A fan game based on the prologue of "The Way of Kings" by Brandon Sanderson, and much of the text is taken or paraphrased from the book. The narration line at the beginning is from Michael Kramer and Kate Reading's official audiobook recording for the book.
