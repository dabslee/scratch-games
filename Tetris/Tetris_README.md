# Tetris

## Instructions
- Click the green flag to start.
- Use the left, right, and down arrow keys to shift the falling piece left, right, or down respectively.
- Press the up arrow or X to rotate clockwise or Z to rotate counterclockwise.
- Press space to hard drop a piece straight down.
- Press C to hold the current piece for later. A preview of the next three pieces are shown on the right.
- Fill a whole row to clear it. Every 10 lines the level goes up and pieces fall faster.
- When the stack reaches the top it's game over. See how far you can get!
